# Knowledge library provenance

The application keeps its existing engine and localization content. The new
library indexes it by stable IDs; it does not claim that synthesized gate or
line paragraphs are verbatim Ra Uru Hu material or an independently licensed
edition. Reading text remains in the existing `natalengine` and `src/locales`
adapters, not in this index.

## Structural references

- [Jovian Archive Human Design Dictionary](https://jovianarchive.com/pages/human-design-dictionary): definitions of activation, channel, circuit, circuit group, harmonic gate, profile and definition. It identifies 36 channels, three principal circuit groups and six ordinary circuits.
- [Jovian Archive, What is Circuitry?](https://jovianarchive.com/pages/what-is-circuitry-in-human-design): group hierarchy and the Integration paths. Integration is shown separately rather than mislabeled as a fourth circuit group.
- [Jovian Archive, Channels in Human Design](https://jovianarchive.com/pages/channels-in-human-design-the-life-force): both gates are required for a complete channel.
- [Jovian Archive, Group Mechanics: The Penta](https://www.jovianarchive.com/Products/446/Group_Mechanics-_The_Penta): the specialist Penta model is distinct from this app's existing chart-overlay team summary.
- [Jovian Archive Human Design Dictionary](https://jovianarchive.com/pages/human-design-dictionary) and [Introduction to the Human Design System](https://jovianarchive.com/blogs/human-design-basics/introduction-to-the-human-design-system): the Design calculation uses 88° of the Sun's movement before birth. Approximately 88–89 days is an explanation, not a fixed subtraction rule.

These links support the catalog structure and short labels only. They are not a
license to reproduce paid source texts. The imported I Ching and Gene Keys
summaries retain their pre-existing provenance and translation treatment.
Numerical placement accuracy has not been independently certified.
